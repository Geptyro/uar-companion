---
title: Replays are noticed as they land
type: improvement
area: uploads
---
The replay folders are watched directly rather than swept on a timer, so a
finished game is picked up in seconds instead of within the next half minute —
and the folders stop being walked twice a minute for the rest of the day,
which on Windows means every check no longer goes through the virus scanner
and, if your Documents live in OneDrive, the sync engine.

---
title: Quiet while you are in a lobby or a game
type: improvement
area: presence
---
Notifications are now held back while you are in a lobby or a game — that is
the moment an interruption costs the most, and it is the moment the app used
to be loudest about someone else flagging ready.

Nothing is queued for later: who was ready mid-game is not news when the game
ends, and the site shows the live roster anyway. If you would rather hear about
it regardless, Settings → Notifications has **Also notify while I'm in a lobby
or a game**, off to begin with.
